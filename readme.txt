 EvroHQ Downloader  -  v2.3.3
 A simple, modern Windows app to download audio or video from
 YouTube and SoundCloud.
====================================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS PACKAGE
----------------------
  EvroHQ-Downloader-Setup-2.3.3      (installer)
  EvroHQ-Downloader-Portable-2.3.3   (portable, no install)
  That's all you need. Just run it.
  yt-dlp and ffmpeg are already inside - nothing else to install.

HOW TO USE
----------
  1. Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link (video, track, or playlist).
     The border turns green when it's valid; a preview appears
     on the right.

  2. Pick a format:
       YouTube:
         - Audio -> WAV (lossless) or MP3 (320 kbps)
         - Video -> MP4 up to 1080p / 2K / 4K
         - Compatible (H.264 + AAC) -> 1080p max, no re-encode
       SoundCloud:
         - Audio only -> Original or WAV

  3. Optional (YouTube):
       - Trim a time range (single videos).
       - Split by chapters when the video has chapters.
       - Embed metadata & cover art on audio (cover art needs MP3).
       - For a playlist, pick the tracks you want. Files land in
         a folder named after the playlist.

  4. Choose an output folder (defaults to your Downloads).

  5. Click DOWNLOAD.
     On a playlist, the status shows 2/12 so you know where you are.

THEMES
------
  7 built-in themes to match your setup. Your choice is remembered
  between sessions.

  Dark:
    - EvroHQ (default)
    - Graphite & Amber
    - Carbon & Cyan
    - Obsidian & Violet
    - Charcoal & Signal Red

  Light:
    - Paper & Rust
    - Bone & Forest

  Switch anytime from the theme selector in the app.

UPDATES
-------
  On launch, if an update for yt-dlp or the app itself is
  available, install it to benefit from the latest bug fixes,
  performance improvements, and compatibility with YouTube's
  ongoing changes.

  The app updates itself - when a new version is available, a
  banner appears at the top of the window. Click "Update now".
  The rest of the app stays locked until you do.

SECURITY NOTE
-------------
  This app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen or your
  antivirus may show a warning ("unknown publisher" / false positive,
  common with download tools). The app is safe and open source - you
  can read the full source code at the official link below.

  If needed, click "More info" -> "Run anyway", or allow it in your
  antivirus.
